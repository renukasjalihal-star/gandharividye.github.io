About the class

The third eye is the point between the brows that many traditions use as a focus for awareness. In this class you learn to settle your attention there, step by step, in a calm and unhurried setting. No prior experience is needed.

Concentration

Train your attention to stay steady and focused.

Memory power

Build a calmer, clearer mind that holds on to what you learn.

Intuition

Listen more closely to your inner voice and first instincts.

The 21-day challenge

Practise every day for three weeks. Each day builds on the last, so the habit sticks and the results become something you can feel.

Three weeks of guided daily practice, with support throughout.
